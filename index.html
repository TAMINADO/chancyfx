The Company

A forex company built on process, not hype.

TAMINADO FX exists to raise the standard of retail and professional forex trading through education, research and mentorship. We teach how markets move, how to manage risk, and how to build the discipline that makes results repeatable.

  • Risk first. Protect capital before chasing profit.
  • Structure over noise. Clear, testable rules for analysis and entries.
  • Process over outcome. Judge performance on execution.
  • Transparency. Education only, with honest risk disclosure.

THE CURRICULUMProgressive learning pathway
01 / FOUNDATIONS

Forex Market Framework

Market participants, currency pairs, sessions, quotations and the mechanics of price movement.

Outcome · Understand the environment in which price develops.
02 / PRICE READING

Candlestick Analysis

OHLC structure, candle context, rejection, displacement and the limitations of single-candle signals.

Outcome · Read candles in context rather than isolation.
03 / STRUCTURE

Market Structure & Trend Logic

Swing highs and lows, breaks of structure, changes of character, trend phases and ranges.

Outcome · Map directional and non-directional conditions.
04 / KEY AREAS

Support, Resistance & Zones

Key price areas, supply and demand concepts, multi-timeframe context and level validation.

Outcome · Build a consistent chart-markup process.
05 / LIQUIDITY

Liquidity & Stop Clusters

Equal highs and lows, obvious reference points, liquidity pools and common interpretation errors.

Outcome · Recognise liquidity concepts without treating them as certainty.
06 / IMBALANCE

Fair Value Gaps & Displacement

Imbalance, fair value gaps, impulsive movement, mitigation ideas and contextual limitations.

Outcome · Classify imbalances and record objective observations.
07 / SMC I

Smart Money Concepts: Core Framework

Order blocks, breaker blocks, mitigation blocks and the relationship between structure and liquidity.

Outcome · Explain the main SMC terms and how they relate.
08 / SMC II

SMC Multi-Timeframe Analysis

Top-down context, dealing ranges, premium and discount, confluence and scenario documentation.

Outcome · Build a repeatable multi-timeframe analysis template.
09 / CRT

Candle Range Theory

Reference-candle ranges, range expansion, sweep-and-reclaim interpretations and timeframe context.

Outcome · Identify and document CRT patterns for study.
10 / EXECUTION STUDY

Entry Models & Trade Management

Confirmation criteria, invalidation, scenario planning, trade review and execution quality.

Outcome · Define rules clearly before evaluating examples.
11 / RISK & PROCESS

Risk, Probability & Psychology

Position sizing principles, drawdown, expectancy, sample size, discipline and journaling.

Outcome · Evaluate process quality and understand uncertainty.
12 / CAPSTONE

Build & Validate Your Trading Plan

Research questions, historical review, demo observation, record keeping and ongoing refinement.

Outcome · Produce a written, testable study plan.

Risk Basics Quiz 0 / 3

Pre-Trade Checklist

  • Is the trend / structure clear on a higher timeframe?
  • Is my entry at a key level with confirmation?
  • Is my stop placed at an invalidation point?
  • Do I risk 1–2% or less of my account?
  • Is the reward at least 2x the risk?
  • Is major news due? Am I calm and following my plan?
